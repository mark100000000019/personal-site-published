Weather text generation

A job advertised as 50% science, 50% programming — in practice it was close to 100% programming, and it's what completed my move from scientist to programmer. The main work was turning structured weather data into natural-language forecast text under hard constraints, for example a forecast that had to fit inside a single SMS message. This was pre-LLM, rule-based text generation: fixed templates and branching rules, not a statistical model.
