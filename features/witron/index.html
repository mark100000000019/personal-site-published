Witron

I spent time working at Witron, a distribution centre logistics company. Stock arrives by lorry, gets put away into storage, is picked against an order, then loaded onto an outbound lorry and leaves for a store. The animation below loops that journey continuously, with a live log alongside it narrating each step as it happens.

  2. 08:05 — Pallet unloaded, staged for putaway
  3. 08:11 — Stored — bay B-14
  4. 08:12 — Inbound lorry departs
  5. 09:47 — Pick generated — order #4521
  6. 09:49 — Picked from bay B-14
  7. 09:52 — Staged for outbound loading
  8. 10:15 — Outbound lorry arrives — Dock 7
  9. 10:18 — Loaded onto outbound lorry
  10. 10:22 — Departs for store
